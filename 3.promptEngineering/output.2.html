1. **Hydration** – Aim for ≥ 3 L water/day; add electrolytes on long run days.  
2. **Timing** – Carbohydrate‑rich meals are placed around workouts (pre‑ and post‑run) to replenish glycogen.  
3. **Protein Distribution** – ~20‑30 g of high‑quality protein each feeding supports muscle‑protein synthesis.  
4. **Micronutrients** – The variety of fruits, vegetables, and nuts supplies iron, calcium, magnesium, and antioxidants crucial for recovery.  
5. **Budget‑Friendly Swaps** –  
   * Oats, rice, and potatoes are low‑cost staples.  
   * Canned salmon or frozen fish can replace fresh salmon occasionally.  
   * Bulk‑buy whey isolate and Greek yogurt for cheaper protein sources.
